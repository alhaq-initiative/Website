About Al-Haq Initiative

What is Al-Haq Initiative?

Al-Haq Initiative (مبادرة الحق) is a personal initiative for “Seeking the Truth” — truth in history and protection in the present. It publishes free academic research, booklets, and digital tools. Learn more on the About page.

Who runs Al-Haq Initiative?

Afrasyaab Meranai — known casually as Habibur Rahman — is the sole founder, developer, and writer. He owns and operates both ADS Solutions (his UK sole-trader business) and the Al-Haq Initiative brand.

Is Al-Haq Initiative a charity, non-profit, or organization?

No. Al-Haq Initiative is not a charity, non-profit, NGO, or registered organization. It is a personal effort hosted under ADS Solutions (UK sole trader). The founder may later convert it into a registered organization, but that has not happened yet.

Quran Hub

How do I use Quran Hub?

Open Quran Hub, choose a surah, then use the reading controls to jump to an ayah, change font size, resume your last session, browse translations, and listen to recitation.

Can I use Quran Hub on Windows, phone, and browsers?

Yes. Quran Hub works across desktop browsers, Windows laptops, Android phones, iPhone, iPad, and tablets. Chrome and Edge usually offer the best install-as-app experience on desktop and Android. On iPhone and iPad, use Safari and choose Add to Home Screen from the Share menu for an app-like experience.

Can I install Quran Hub like an app?

If your browser supports it, Quran Hub can be installed as a web app. On Windows, Edge and Chrome usually provide the cleanest app window and taskbar shortcut. On iPhone and iPad, open Quran Hub in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.

Does Quran Hub work offline?

Core Quran Hub reader assets are cached after use so returning visits are faster and basic reading support remains available offline. For the best experience, open the page once while connected before relying on offline access.
