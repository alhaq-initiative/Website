Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions and get help with our services.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use Taleem AI?

Go to the Library or Projects page, select Taleem AI, and follow the instructions to try the demo and receive real-time feedback on your recitation.

How can I donate to support your work?

Visit our Donate page for secure online donations via Ko-fi or other available methods.

How do I contact you for support or collaboration?

Use the Contact page to send us a message directly, or email us at contact@alhaq.digital.

Is my data safe?

Yes, we take privacy seriously and do not share your data with third parties. All information is handled securely.

Can I access the infographics and book chapters for free?

Yes, all infographic chapters and resources in the Library are freely accessible to all users.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Please use the Contact page or email us with details about the bug or your suggestion. We appreciate your feedback!

What is Alhaq Digital Services' mission?

Our mission is to provide accessible Islamic resources, educational tools, and digital services to empower communities and foster learning.

Are there plans to add more books or features?

Yes, we are continuously working to expand our Library and add new features. Stay tuned for updates!

How do I access the Quran and Hadith sections?

Go to the Library page and select the Quran or Hadith section to explore translations, tafsir, and authentic collections.
