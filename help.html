Frequently Asked Questions

About Alhaq Digital Services (ADS)

What is Alhaq Digital Services?

Alhaq Digital Services (ADS) provides accessible Islamic resources, educational tools, and digital services to empower communities and foster learning. Learn more on our About page.

What is Alhaq Digital Services' mission?

Our mission is to provide accessible Islamic resources, educational tools, and digital services to empower communities and foster learning. See our About section for more details.

Library

How can I access the library? Is it free?

Yes, all infographics and most resources in the Library are freely accessible to all users. Visit the Library page to explore the available content.

How do I access the Quran and Hadith sections?

Go to the Library page and select the Quran or Hadith section to explore translations, tafsir, and authentic collections.

Usage

How do I use Taleem AI?

Download our App (Taleem AI) from Google Play Store or App Store. Once installed, follow the instructions to receive real-time feedback on your recitation.

Donations

How can I donate to support your work?

Visit our Donate page for secure online donations via Ko-fi or other available methods.

Contact & Support

How do I contact you for support or collaboration?

Use the Contact page, or click the floating button on the bottom right corner of the webpage, to send us a message directly.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Please use the Contact page or email us with details about the bug or your suggestion. We appreciate your feedback!

Privacy & Data

Is my data safe?

Yes, we take privacy seriously and do not share your data with third parties. All information is handled securely. For more details, see our Contact page to reach out with privacy concerns.

Features & Updates

Yes, we are continuously working to expand our Library and add new features. Stay tuned for updates!
