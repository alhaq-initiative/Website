Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection are fundamental to our Islamic values of trust (Amanah) and responsibility.

Last Updated: August 5, 2025

Introduction

DeenShield is committed to protecting your privacy while providing you with the best Islamic productivity experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information in accordance with Islamic principles of trust (Amanah) and transparency.

Information We Collect

DeenShield is designed with privacy-first principles. We collect minimal information necessary to provide our services:

  • Location Data (Optional): Used only for accurate prayer times calculation based on your geographical location
  • Usage Preferences: App settings, dhikr counts, Quran reading progress, and task management data
  • Device Information: Basic device type and browser information for compatibility purposes
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to enhance your spiritual and productivity journey:

  • Calculate accurate prayer times for your specific location
  • Save your dhikr progress and spiritual achievements
  • Bookmark Quran verses and track reading progress
  • Manage your Islamic productivity goals and tasks
  • Customize your dashboard and widget preferences
  • Provide relevant Islamic content and reminders

Data Storage & Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

  • Local Storage Priority: Most of your data is stored locally on your device
  • Encryption: All data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties
  • Secure Infrastructure: Our servers use advanced security measures and regular updates
  • Data Minimization: We only store what's necessary for app functionality
  • Regular Audits: Our security practices are regularly reviewed and updated

Islamic Content Sources

We ensure the authenticity and accuracy of all Islamic content:

  • Quran text sourced from verified Islamic organizations and institutions
  • Prayer time calculations using established Islamic astronomical methods
  • Dhikr and Dua content verified against classical Islamic texts
  • All religious content reviewed by qualified Islamic scholars
  • Regular updates to maintain accuracy and authenticity

Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal tracking technologies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic app functionality (login state, preferences)
  • No Advertising Cookies: We do not use cookies for advertising or marketing
  • Anonymous Analytics: Basic usage patterns to improve app performance (can be disabled)
  • No Cross-Site Tracking: We do not track your activity across other websites
  • User Control: You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings

Your Rights & Control

You have complete control over your data and privacy:

  • Data Access: View all data stored about you within the app
  • Data Deletion: Permanently delete your data at any time
  • Data Export: Export your dhikr progress, bookmarks, and settings
  • Location Control: Enable or disable location services anytime
  • Notification Control: Manage prayer time and reminder notifications
  • Data Portability: Transfer your data to other Islamic apps if desired

Children's Privacy

DeenShield is designed to be family-friendly and safe for users of all ages:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Parents can monitor and control their children's app usage
  • All content is appropriate for family viewing
  • If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us

International Users

DeenShield serves Muslims worldwide:

  • Data protection complies with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations
  • Prayer times calculated for locations worldwide
  • Multi-language support for diverse Muslim communities
  • Respect for local privacy laws and cultural considerations

Contact Us

For privacy concerns, questions, or data requests:

  • Email: privacy@alhaqds.software
  • Support: support@alhaqds.software
  • Website: alhaqds.software
  • Response Time: Within 24 hours for privacy-related inquiries

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements to DeenShield or changes in privacy regulations. Users will be notified of significant changes through:

  • In-app notifications for major updates
  • Email notifications (if you've provided an email)
  • Website announcements
  • Updated "Last Modified" date at the top of this policy
