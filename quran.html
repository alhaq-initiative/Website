Tafsir & Commentary

Ibn Kathir Tafsir

One of the most comprehensive and respected tafsir works in Islamic scholarship.

Al-Fatiha Tafsir

"The Opening" - This surah is the foundation of the Quran and teaches us the proper way to address Allah in prayer.

Al-Ikhlas Tafsir

"The Sincerity" - This surah is equivalent to one-third of the Quran and emphasizes the pure monotheism of Islam.

Ya-Sin Tafsir

"The Heart of the Quran" - This surah addresses the truth of the Quran and the mission of the Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ).

Contemporary Tafsir

Modern interpretations that address contemporary issues and challenges.

Ma'ariful Quran

A comprehensive modern tafir that explains the Quran in light of contemporary understanding.

Tafsir Al-Tabari

One of the earliest and most comprehensive tafir works, covering historical context and linguistic analysis.

Tafsir Al-Qurtubi

A detailed tafir that focuses on legal rulings and practical applications of Quranic verses.
