About Us

Alhaq Initiative is dedicated to fusing technology with the timeless wisdom of Islam. Our mission is to create digital tools and resources that empower the community, foster learning, and strengthen faith in the modern world.

Our Mission

To develop innovative, spiritually enriching digital solutions that help users deepen their knowledge and practice of Islam.

Our Vision

To be a leading provider of Islamic digital services, making authentic knowledge and resources accessible to all.

Our Values

  • Integrity & Authenticity
  • Community Empowerment
  • Innovation & Excellence
  • Faith-Centered Approach

Founder

Afrasyaab Meranai — known casually as Habibur Rahman — is the founder, developer, and writer behind the Alhaq Initiative. He is the sole owner of both ADS Solutions (UK sole trader) and the public-facing Alhaq Initiative brand.

  • Software, products & services are built and delivered under ADS Solutions (his sole-trader business).
  • Academic and literary work — historical research, translations, and booklets — is authored personally by the founder.

The Alhaq Initiative is not a separate legal entity, charity, or organization. It is the founder’s personal mission hosted under ADS Solutions. Donations and sponsorships go to the founder as a UK sole trader; he commits, as a Muslim, to spend that income on the initiative’s projects.
