About the Al-Haq Initiative

The Al-Haq Initiative is dedicated to seeking truth in history and protecting health in the present. Our work fuses rigorous primary-source research with digital tools that empower the community.

Our Mission

At Al-Haq Studio, we believe in using technology to make a positive impact. That is why all of our free tools and community solutions are offered directly through the Al-Haq Initiative—our dedicated mission to provide free, high-quality digital tools to the public, entirely built and backed by our studio.

We develop innovative, spiritually enriching digital solutions and historical research that help the community deepen knowledge, strengthen practice, and build resilience.

Our Vision

To be a trusted source of verified historical knowledge and protection tools, making authentic research and community-centered technology accessible to all.

Our Values

  • check_circle Truth in Sources: Rigorous verification through primary documents, never political distortion.
  • check_circle Community Empowerment: Tools and resources that strengthen identity, scholarship, and wellbeing.
  • check_circle Islamic Ethics: All content and technology reflects Islamic values and protects against harm.
  • check_circle Free & Accessible: All research and core resources available free, no paywalls.

About the Founder & Operational Structure

Al-Haq Studio and the Al-Haq Initiative were founded by, and are wholly owned and operated by, Habibur Rahman Meranai as a UK sole trader. Al-Haq Studio functions as the commercial software development studio, while the Al-Haq Initiative functions as the non-profit digital welfare and historical research wing, with all software, research platforms, and infrastructure engineered and maintained by Habibur Rahman Meranai through Al-Haq Studio.

  • • Software, products & services are engineered and delivered under Al-Haq Studio, his commercial sole-trader business.
  • • Academic & literary works, including historical research, verified booklets, and translations, are authored personally by Habibur Rahman Meranai.

The Al-Haq Initiative is not a registered charity or separate corporate entity; it operates as the digital welfare and community research wing of Al-Haq Studio.
