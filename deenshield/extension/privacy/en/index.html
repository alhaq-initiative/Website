DeenShield Extension – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-19

Overview

The DeenShield browser extension is designed to enhance your browsing experience with Islamic-friendly features. We respect your privacy. This extension does not sell your data, and aims to minimize data collection.

Data Collection

  • No account is required to use the extension.
  • No personal data is sent to our servers by default.
  • Some settings may be stored locally in your browser (extension storage) to remember your preferences.

Permissions

The extension may request limited permissions strictly required for its features. For example, access to the active tab to apply content filtering, or storage permission to save preferences. We do not access data beyond what is necessary for functionality.

Third-Party Services

The extension does not embed third-party trackers. If future features integrate optional services (e.g., Quran audio), they will be clearly disclosed and limited to what’s needed.

Security

We follow best practices to protect your data, including the principle of least privilege for extension permissions and enforcing secure connections on our websites.
