Local-First Philosophy

We prioritize on-device processing and storage whenever practical to reduce centralized data retention risk. This section will be expanded with implementation notes (caching strategies, offline behavior, and explicit non-collection assertions) in future revisions.

Form Handling & Submission Flow

Contact and feedback forms are submitted to a secure Logic App endpoint and processed transiently. We do not build marketing profiles; messages are used solely for direct response and improvement tracking. Spam mitigation includes a honeypot field and selective rate limiting.

Ethical & Faith-Aligned Standards

Core principles: truthful representation, privacy stewardship, non-extractive design, avoidance of dark patterns, and alignment with Islamic ethical guidance. Enforcement details and escalation process will be documented here in a future expansion.
