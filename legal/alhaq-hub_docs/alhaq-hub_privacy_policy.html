Overview

Al-Haq Hub is a digital productivity suite developed by Al-Haq Studio for the Al-Haq Initiative to provide faith-aligned daily utilities in a clean, distraction-free environment. We design all features to function locally on your device whenever possible. This policy explains what information we process, why we process it, and your controls.

Scope

This policy applies to Al-Haq Hub pages hosted by the Al-Haq Initiative and to Al-Haq Hub application clients (including Android and web platforms) referencing this document. It does not cover third-party websites or services that may link to or integrate with our tools.

Information We Process

Al-Haq Hub adheres to a strict local-by-default, data-minimisation architecture. Depending on feature usage, the application processes:

  • Application Preferences: Theme selection, language choices, notification settings, and calculation methods, stored strictly on-device.
  • Prayer & Qibla Inputs: Approximate or precise device coordinates (when permission is granted) and system time zone to compute solar angles and compass bearings locally.
  • Voluntary Feedback: Comments and correspondence email addresses you explicitly provide when submitting inquiries or beta feedback.
  • Local Diagnostic Data: Ephemeral system logs generated locally when debugging reported issues, never transmitted automatically.

We do not sell personal data, we do not track browsing history, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Device Permissions (Android Application)

  • Location: Used exclusively to compute astronomical prayer times and Qibla direction on-device. You can revoke this permission at any time in system settings; the application will fall back to manual city selection.
  • Notifications: Used to trigger local prayer alerts and opt-in reminders. Controlled directly through app settings and OS notification channels.
  • Compass & Orientation: Used strictly during active Qibla compass viewing to calibrate direction.

How We Use Information

  • Deliver core utility functions (astronomical calculations, Qibla compass, local reminders) and personalise UI settings.
  • Respond directly to user-initiated support requests and bug reports.
  • Maintain platform integrity and comply with statutory obligations under UK law.

On-Device Processing & Storage

All mathematical calculations, astronomical algorithms, and prayer calendars execute directly on your local device hardware. Clearing your application storage or browser cache removes all locally saved preferences and history immediately.

Cookies & Local Storage (Web)

Our static web pages use only first-party local storage to remember your preferred language, dark mode theme, and reading progress. We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, fingerprinting scripts, or cross-site trackers.

Your Rights & Controls

In compliance with the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR, you maintain complete rights to:

  • Access, rectify, or request deletion of any correspondence data held in our support records.
  • Revoke device permissions (Location, Notifications, Sensors) at any time.
  • Export or purge all local application data directly through application settings.
