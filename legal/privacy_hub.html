Core Privacy Architecture

Zero-Telemetry & No Tracking

We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral analytics, or session recording scripts in our applications. User activity is never monetised, profiled, or sold to third parties.

100% Tracking-Free

On-Device AI Inference

Visual content filtering (AmniHaze via LiteRT and TensorFlow Lite) processes all video streams and images directly on your local hardware. Zero screen frames, pixels, or images are transmitted across the network or saved remotely.

On-Device Neural Models

Offline Cryptographic Licensing

Supporter Pass validation uses asymmetric NIST P-256 ECDSA public key verification. License keys are validated locally on the device without requiring persistent internet connections or central phone-home checkpoints.

NIST P-256 ECDSA

Data Minimisation & Ephemeral Handling

Web contact forms, newsletter signups, and beta registrations collect strictly essential contact fields. Submissions are processed securely for correspondence and purged in accordance with our retention policy.
