Private by design

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t clutter your worship with ads. DeenHub runs core features on‑device and stores only what’s needed for your experience.

  • Location usage stays on your phone for prayer time/Qibla calculation.
  • Opt‑in reminders with simple controls and quiet hours.
  • Clear data controls: reset or export, anytime.

Frequently asked questions

Is DeenHub free?

During beta, DeenHub is free. We may offer a free core with optional supporter features later to sustain the project.

Which platforms are supported?

iOS and Android are targeted for launch. Beta invites roll out in waves as we expand testing.

Does it work offline?

Yes—core features like stored prayer times, Qibla (with cached calibration), and adhkaar browsing work without internet.

How accurate are the prayer times?

We support multiple established calculation methods with offsets for local adjustments. You’re in control.
