Quran Hub

AI-powered Quran recitation feedback and memorization practice. Quran Hub is now a feature inside the DeenHub mobile app (with an upcoming companion web app) — bringing real-time correction, moving highlight, and word-by-word analysis into the same daily-deen companion you already use for prayer times, Qibla, and adhkaar.

Under the hood, Quran Hub is powered by our in-house AI engine, TaleemAI. DeenHub is the mobile app, Quran Hub is the feature inside it, and TaleemAI is the engine that makes the real-time recitation feedback possible.

  • Real-time recitation correction and feedback — powered by TaleemAI
  • Moving highlight and word-by-word analysis
  • Multiple surahs and full-surah display
  • Memorization practice with repetition and backward-correction recognition
  • Ships inside DeenHub mobile (Android beta) · upcoming web app companion
