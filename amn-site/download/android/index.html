AmnShield for Android

Install AmnShield on Android via Google Play or join the closed testing track for pre-release builds. The app is designed for privacy-first, on-device protection and focus workflows.

Install from Google Play Join Closed Testing Request Android Support

What You Get

  • App blocking and schedule controls
  • Keyword filtering and focus sessions
  • On-device processing for sensitive checks

System Requirements

  • Android 8.0 or higher
  • Accessibility permission enabled
  • Stable internet for Play install/update

Recommended Setup

  • Install app from Play
  • Enable required permissions
  • Configure blockers in Home and Blocks tabs

Closed Testing Steps

  1. Open the testing opt-in link.
  2. Accept invitation with your Play account.
  3. Install/update from Play after enrollment.

If enrollment is full or unavailable, contact support to be added to the next cohort.
